On iPhone · 在 iPhone 上使用

Open the published website link in Safari, then choose a guide. A complete reading view is also included for file previews that cannot run interactive features.

在 Safari 中打开已发布的网站链接，再选择指南。如果文件预览无法运行互动功能，也可以使用内置的完整阅读版。

English and Chinese editions of the same guide share progress in the same browser and website. Videos retain their original audio; subtitle availability depends on the publisher.

同一指南的中英文版，在同一浏览器和同一网站中共用学习进度。视频保留原版音频；是否提供中文字幕由发布者决定。
